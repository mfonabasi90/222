Methodology
Each photo's head pose (roll/yaw/pitch) is estimated from 68 facial landmarks against a canonical 3D head model; landmarks are then rotated/scaled into an upright, centered "canonical" frame so that tilted photos measure the same as straight ones. Every geometric metric carries a bootstrap uncertainty (±95% CI). Comparison converts each metric's residual (observed difference beyond measurement noise and — when enabled — expected developmental change) into a weighted log-likelihood ratio; weights favour identity-stable traits (canthal tilt, jaw angle…) and penalise expression/styling-sensitive ones (mouth openness, brows). Results are heuristic, not forensic identification.
